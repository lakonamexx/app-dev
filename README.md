# Coraline (2009)

## About the Movie

**Coraline** is a *stop-motion animated fantasy and horror movie* released in 2009. It was directed by **Henry Selick** and based on the book written by Neil Gaiman.

## Story Summary

The story follows a young girl named **Coraline Jones** who moves into a new home with her parents. One day, she discovers a secret door that leads to another world where everything seems perfect.

However, Coraline soon discovers that the **Other Mother** has evil intentions. She must find a way to escape the other world and save her real parents.
